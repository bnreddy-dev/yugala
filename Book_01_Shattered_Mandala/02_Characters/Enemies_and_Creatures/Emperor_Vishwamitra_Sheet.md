# CHARACTER SHEET: EMPEROR VISHWAMITRA (THE ANTAGONIST)

## IDENTITY
* **Name:** Vishwamitra (Meaning: Friend of the World—a dark twist on his despotic rule).
* **Role:** The Architect of Absolute Peace / Emperor of Aryavarta.
* **Age:** Appears 40 (Actually over 150 due to stolen Punya).

## PERSONALITY
* **Strengths:** Absolute conviction, genius-level intellect, master manipulator.
* **Weaknesses:** Absolute arrogance, refuses to believe chaos has any value.
* **Flaws:** He believes the end always justifies the means.
* **Motivation:** He witnessed the devastation of the great war and vowed to never let humanity have the freedom to destroy themselves again.

## VISUAL IDENTITY
* **Face:** Perfectly symmetrical, calm, unsettlingly serene. Never shows anger.
* **Hair:** Shaved bald, painted with intricate, glowing golden geometric tattoos (symbols of control).
* **Body Type:** Tall, imposing, perfectly postured.
* **Clothing:** Immaculate white silk robes lined with heavy gold armor plating. Zero dirt, zero wrinkles.
* **Weapon:** The Brahmashira (a dormant, reality-altering Astra housed in a levitating golden lotus).
* **Signature Mark:** His eyes emit a soft, blinding white light (no pupils), representing his blinding vision of 'truth'.

## CHARACTER ARC
* **Starting Point:** The unquestioned ruler imposing perfect, sterile peace.
* **Conflict:** Encounters Aryan, a wild element of chaos he cannot predict or control.
* **Final Transformation:** His perfect facade shatters, revealing a terrified, desperate tyrant underneath.

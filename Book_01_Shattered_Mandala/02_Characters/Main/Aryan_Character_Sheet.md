# CHARACTER SHEET: ARYAN (THE PROTAGONIST)

## IDENTITY
* **Name:** Aryan (Meaning: The Noble One, ironically used as he is a disgraced outcast).
* **Role:** The Exiled Ascetic / Reluctant Dharma-Defender.
* **Age:** 26
* **Origin:** The ruins of the Brahma Wound.

## PERSONALITY
* **Strengths:** Unbreakable willpower, masterful hand-to-hand combat (Tapasya-trained).
* **Weaknesses:** Deeply cynical, struggles to trust others, fears failing a second time.
* **Flaws:** He will sacrifice his own physical well-being without hesitation, making him self-destructive.
* **Motivation:** He wants to destroy the remaining Astras because one killed his mentor.

## VISUAL IDENTITY
* **Face:** Sharp jawline, piercing dark eyes with heavy *kage* (shadows) underneath from lack of sleep.
* **Hair:** Long, unruly, tied in a loose, frayed topknot. Jet black.
* **Body Type:** Lean, heavily scarred, athletic (built from ascetic starvation and extreme physical training).
* **Clothing:** Tattered, dark indigo ascetic robes over traditional martial pants. Wrapped forearms.
* **Weapon:** He refuses to use Astras. He fights with a heavy, blunt iron staff wrapped in prayer beads (Kanabo/Lathi hybrid).
* **Signature Mark:** A burn scar on his left shoulder in the shape of a shattered mandala (from surviving an Astra blast).

## CHARACTER ARC
* **Starting Point:** A cynical survivor who believes Dharma is a lie.
* **Lowest Point:** Having to use the very weapon he hates (an Astra) to save an innocent, burning his own soul.
* **Final Transformation:** Embracing his duty, sacrificing himself to shatter the Emperor's illusion.

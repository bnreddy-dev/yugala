# MASTER PRE-PRODUCTION BIBLE: SHATTERED MANDALA
**Status:** LOCKED for Production.

## 1. STORY & LORE
**Premise:** In the mythological sci-fi era of the next Yuga, the Brahma Wound (a crater left by a divine weapon) is scavenged by outcasts. Aryan, a scarred ascetic warrior, guards the ruins from the Kala Chakra Empire and its ruler, Emperor Vishwamitra.
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W). Crisp G-pen, kakeami, screentones.

## 2. MAIN CHARACTERS
**Aryan:** Messy topknot, severe chest/face scars, tattered indigo robes. (Reference: Aryan_Master_Turnaround.jpg)
**Emperor Vishwamitra:** Bald, glowing geometric head tattoos, immaculate white silk, gold armor. (Reference: Emperor_Master_Turnaround.jpg)

## 3. WEAPONS & ENVIRONMENTS
**Kanabo (Aryan's Staff):** Heavy studded iron monk staff wrapped in prayer beads. (Reference: weapon_aryan_staff.jpg)
**Brahma Wound:** Giant cracked stone pillars, ancient Indian temple ruins. (Reference: environment_brahma_wound.jpg)

# UNIVERSE BIBLE: SHATTERED MANDALA

## PHASE 0: PROJECT DEFINITION
* **Identity:** A dark mythic fantasy where divine weapons are radioactive curses, and righteousness is paid for in blood.
* **Genre:** Dark Fantasy & Mythic Folklore (with Cosmic Puranic scale).
* **Core Theme:** The devastating cost of upholding Dharma in a world that rewards Adharma.
* **Philosophical Promise:** Good people will suffer, power corrupts the soul, and ultimate victory requires total sacrifice.

## PHASE 3: WORLD CONCEPT
* **The Era:** The Twilight of the Third Yuga. A post-cataclysmic golden age.
* **Geography:** The continent of Aryavarta, fractured by a massive, ancient blast crater known as the 'Brahma Wound.'
* **Society:** Ruled by warrior clans who hoard ancient technology and spiritual artifacts. The poor live in the shadows of colossal, ruined temples.

## PHASE 4: WORLD HISTORY
One hundred years ago, a catastrophic war was fought using *Astras* (divine celestial weapons). The weapons were so powerful they tore the fabric of reality (the Mandala). The gods abandoned the realm in disgust. Now, mortals dig through the ashes to find dormant Astras, unaware that using them slowly burns away their humanity.

## PHASE 5: SUPERNATURAL SYSTEM (THE ASTRAS)
* **The Power:** Astras are not just weapons; they are sentient energy constructs.
* **The Cost (Karma-Drain):** To use an Astra, a human must pay with their *Punya* (spiritual merit). Overuse causes 'Soul-Burn'—the user physically crystallizes into black salt.

## PHASE 6: STORY CORE
* **Central Conflict:** A disgraced ascetic warrior discovers the Emperor is trying to awaken the ultimate Astra to rewrite reality and erase free will, claiming it will bring 'absolute peace.'
* **Philosophical Conflict:** Is a world of chaotic free will (with suffering) better than a world of absolute, controlled peace (with no freedom)?

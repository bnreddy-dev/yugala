# COMIC SCRIPT: SCENE 02 (PAGE 03)

## PAGE 03
**Page Purpose:** Introduce the Antagonist and his environment. Contrast the dirty ruins with sterile perfection.
**Visual Objective:** Perfect symmetry, clean lines, glowing white/gold.
**Emotional Objective:** Cold, oppressive authority.

**PANEL 1 (Wide horizontal):**
The Emperor Vishwamitra stands on a flawless white marble balcony overlooking the Kala Chakra Citadel. The architecture is perfectly symmetrical, terrifyingly pristine. 
*CAPTION (Emperor):* "Chaos is the disease of the mortal mind."

**PANEL 2 (Close-up):**
Close up of the Emperor's face. His head is bald, covered in intricate, glowing geometric tattoos. He looks calm, arrogant, and unsettlingly serene. 
*CAPTION (Emperor):* "Dharma is not found in freedom."
*CAPTION (Emperor):* "It is found in perfect, absolute control."

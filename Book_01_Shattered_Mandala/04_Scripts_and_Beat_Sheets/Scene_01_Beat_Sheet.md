# BEAT SHEET: THE SUNLESS AWAKENING (SCENE 01)

1. Where are we? 
The Brahma Wound. A colossal crater containing the ruins of the Mahastambha Temple.

2. Who is present? 
Aryan (Protagonist) and a squad of three Emperor's scavengers.

3. What does the reader see? 
A massive establishing shot of the temple ruins. The scavengers are extracting a dormant, glowing Astra from the rubble.

4. What changes? 
Aryan steps out of the shadows, blocking their exit path.

5. What does the character want? 
Aryan wants to destroy the Astra before they can take it to the Emperor.

6. What prevents them? 
The lead scavenger activates the Astra, mutating his arm into a blade of pure energy.

7. What action occurs? 
A brutal, high-kinetic fight. Aryan uses his heavy iron monk staff to shatter the energy blade using pure physical force (Tapasya) rather than magic.

8. What emotion changes? 
The scavengers go from arrogant to terrified. Aryan remains deadly calm.

9. What information is revealed? 
The Emperor is closer to finding the 'Pasupatastra' than Aryan thought.

10. What question makes the reader continue? 
If Aryan can shatter a celestial weapon with just an iron staff, what kind of dark vow did he take to get that strong?

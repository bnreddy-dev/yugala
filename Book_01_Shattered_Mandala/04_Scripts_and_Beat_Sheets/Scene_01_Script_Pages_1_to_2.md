# COMIC SCRIPT: SCENE 01 (PAGES 1-2)

## PAGE 01 (SPLASH PAGE)
**Page Purpose:** Establish the scale, the ruins, and the tone.
**Visual Objective:** Show the absolute devastation of the Brahma Wound.
**Emotional Objective:** Isolation and forgotten grandeur.

**PANEL 1 (Full Page Splash):**
Wide establishing shot. The crater of the ancient kings. Giant cracked stone pillars of the Mahastambha Temple jut out of the debris. The sky is dark and moody. Tiny silhouettes of three explorers (scavengers) with flashlights are seen at the bottom to show massive scale.
*SFX:* WIND!
*SFX:* ECHO...
*CAPTION (Aryan):* "A hundred years ago, they tore the sky open in the name of Dharma."
*CAPTION (Aryan):* "Now, they pick through the scabs."

---

## PAGE 02
**Page Purpose:** Introduce the threat and the protagonist.
**Visual Objective:** High contrast lighting, revealing Aryan's scarred face.

**PANEL 1 (Horizontal wide):**
Inside the temple ruins. The lead scavenger pulls a glowing, geometric metal cylinder (a dormant Astra) from the rubble. The light casts harsh shadows on his greedy face.
*SFX:* CLINK.
*SCAVENGER:* "The Emperor will pay a kingdom for this core."

**PANEL 2 (Vertical tall):**
A heavy iron staff (Kanabo) slams down onto the stone floor in the foreground, kicking up dust. The scavenger looks up in shock.
*SFX:* KRAK!

**PANEL 3 (Close-up):**
Close up of Aryan's eyes from the shadows. Heavy kage (shadows) under his eyes. Cold, dead stare. 
*ARYAN (Dialogue):* "Put it back."

**PANEL 4 (Action):**
The scavenger panics, twists the cylinder. The cylinder violently expands, fusing into his arm, generating a crackling blade of blinding white energy that breaks the panel border.
*SFX:* BZZZZZKT!
*SCAVENGER (Screaming):* "DIE, ASCETIC!"

# UNIVERSE BIBLE: BOOK 03 ? THE BROKEN KALPA

## 1. COSMIC SETTING: THE BROKEN KALPA (THE LIMINAL VOID)
* **The Physics of the Void:** When the Kala Chakra satellite core cracked, time and space lost linear cohesion. Gravity operates along fractured floating planetary plates. Black-and-gold gravitational lensing tears across the sky like cracked stained glass.
* **The Soma-Transmutation:** Mortals channeling high-frequency Astras risk their living flesh turning into permanent celestial diamond/crystal. Aryan embraces this transformation to withstand the Emperor's singularity attacks.

## 2. FACTIONS & FINAL STAKES
* **The Singularity Imperial Remnant:** Fanatical automatons and corrupted inquisitors seeking to execute Vishwamitra's suicide protocol to cleanse mortal memory.
* **The Free Spirits of Aryavarta:** Outcasts and mortals on the ground looking up as the false sky breaks, awaiting the dawn of true self-determination.

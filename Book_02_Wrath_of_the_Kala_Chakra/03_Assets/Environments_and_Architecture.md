# ENVIRONMENTS & ARCHITECTURAL CODEX: BOOK 02

## 1. THE ASHEN WASTES
* Vast salt flats covered in black volcanic ash. Giant cracked stone pillars from ancient Vedic temple complexes jut out at unnatural angles.

## 2. THE VEDIC UNDERGROUND VAULTS
* Subterranean halls carved into basalt stone, reinforced with heavy rusted iron beams and brass pneumatic piping. Dimly lit by hanging oil lamps and glowing crystal energy jars.

## 3. THE KALA CHAKRA CITADEL
* Inverted black obsidian pyramid suspended in the clouds. Three massive, rotating concentric golden rings encircle its waist, spinning with blinding electric arcs. The interior features grand colonnades of polished white marble and black gold.

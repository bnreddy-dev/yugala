# ASSETS & TECH SPECIFICATIONS: BOOK 02

## 1. WEAPONS
* **Resonant Kanabo:** Octagonal forged iron, 6ft length, 120 lbs mass, internal Astra kinetic amplifier.
* **Pulse Chakrams:** Aerodynamic titanium-alloy rings, 14-inch diameter, magnetic recall coils.
* **Kala-Danda Scepter:** Ornate ceremonial rod, 5ft length, floating zero-point singularity finial.
* **Imperial Vibro-Khanda:** Double-edged straight heavy sword with high-frequency vibrating edge.

## 2. VEHICLES
* **Garuda Sand-Skiff:** Twin turbine light atmospheric vehicle, top speed 320 km/h, armed with magnetic tow cable.
* **Imperial Sky-Barge (Pushpaka-Transport):** Armored troop transport capable of atmospheric-to-orbital ascent, reinforced with heavy deflector fields.

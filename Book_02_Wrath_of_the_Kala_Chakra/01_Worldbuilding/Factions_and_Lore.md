# FACTIONS AND MILITARY DOCTRINE: BOOK 02

## 1. THE IMPERIAL SKY LEGION (KALA CHAKRA GUARD)
* **Command Structure:** Supreme Command: Emperor Vishwamitra -> High General: Kripa -> Sector Centurions -> Legionnaires.
* **Armor & Equipment:** 
  * Heavy articulated composite plate styled after ancient Chola and Maurya battle armor, reinforced with carbon-weave under-suits.
  * Helmets feature stylized demon-visages (*Rakshasa masks*) with mono-eye tracking optics.
  * Standard Armament: Astra-Rifles (firing high-density plasma darts) and electrified Vibro-Khanda blades.

## 2. THE VAYU RESISTANCE (THE ASHEN SECT)
* **Philosophy:** "Through pain we awaken; through freedom we ascend."
* **Tactics:** Guerrilla warfare, electromagnetic pulse traps, high-speed sand skiffs, and magnetic grapple maneuvering.
* **Key Figure:** Veda — Chief Mechanist of the Underground, responsible for maintaining the last functional anti-orbital communication relay.

## 3. WAR ASSETS & BIO-MECHS
* **Vritra-Class Siege Engine:** A six-legged siege titan standing 120 meters tall, housing heavy kinetic catapults and twin beam cannons. Powered by a caged Asura-grade energy beast in its thoracic chamber.
* **Garuda Skiffs:** High-speed twin-turbine scout skiffs equipped with forward plasma repeaters and harpoon tethers.

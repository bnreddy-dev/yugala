# CHARACTER DESIGN SHEET: EMPEROR VISHWAMITRA
**Role:** Primary Antagonist / Sovereign of the Kala Chakra  
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W) — Monumental presence, pristine geometric linework, absolute symmetry.

## 1. VISUAL ANATOMY & COSTUME
* **Physique:** Regal, imposing, motionless and godlike posture.
* **Head & Face:** Completely shaved head inscribed with intricate, glowing geometric mandala tattoos that radiate from his forehead (*Ajna Chakra*). Calm, chilling, unblinking celestial eyes.
* **Attire:**
  * Flowing multi-layered royal silk robes of spotless white and obsidian black.
  * Majestic floating ceremonial pauldrons resembling stylized golden wheel-spokes.
  * Golden breastplate sculpted with celestial astronomical charts.

## 2. WEAPONS & POWERS
* **Weapon:** *Kala-Danda (The Rod of World-Time)* — A slender, ornate scepter tipped with a floating black singularity orb that bends light and gravity around it.
* **Abilities:** Absolute telekinesis, localized gravity manipulation, projection of colossal Astra-avatars, instantaneous space-fold shielding.

## 3. PHILOSOPHY
* "Mortals are cursed by their own freedom. In chaos, they butcher one another. Under my Wheel, they shall know eternal, silent perfection."

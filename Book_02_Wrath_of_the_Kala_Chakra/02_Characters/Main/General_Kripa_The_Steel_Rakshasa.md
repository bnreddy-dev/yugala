# CHARACTER DESIGN SHEET: GENERAL KRIPA (THE STEEL RAKSHASA)
**Role:** Major Boss Antagonist / Commander of Imperial Vanguard  
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W) — Heavy solid black fills (*beta-nuri*), jagged aggressive silhouettes.

## 1. VISUAL ANATOMY & ARMOR
* **Physique:** Massive 8-foot cyber-enhanced juggernaut.
* **Armor:** Colossal full-body iron exoskeleton shaped in the motif of a fierce temple guardian beast (*Rakshasa*). Four exhaust ports on his back vent thick plumes of superheated steam and sparks.
* **Visor:** Slit red optical sensor beneath a horned war helm with ornate Sanskrit carvings.

## 2. WEAPONRY
* **Weapons:** Twin Hydraulic Heavy Gada (War Maces) studded with diamond-carbide teeth that detonate kinetic impact charges upon contact.
* **Combat Style:** Relentless forward momentum, ground-shattering seismic slams, and crushing close-quarters grapples.

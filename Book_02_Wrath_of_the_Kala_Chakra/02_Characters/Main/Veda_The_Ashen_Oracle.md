# CHARACTER DESIGN SHEET: VEDA (THE ASHEN ORACLE)
**Role:** Main Ally / Scavenger Engineer  
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W) — Intricate mechanical detailing, dynamic line weights.

## 1. VISUAL ANATOMY & COSTUME
* **Physique:** Agile, athletic, wiry frame built for acrobatics and high-speed repair.
* **Head & Face:** Sharp youthful features with smudges of carbon grease. Braided ash-gray hair pulled back with brass filigree clips.
* **Cybernetic Enhancement:** Left eye replaced with a triple-lens brass and quartz ocular mechanism (*Trinetra Optic*) that whirs into focus.
* **Attire:**
  * Tailored sand-leather flight jacket with insulated high collar and rolled-up sleeves.
  * Utility harness across chest holding power cells, diagnostic needles, and magnetic coils.
  * Reinforced combat trousers with knee pads and magnetic climbing boots.

## 2. WEAPON & EQUIPMENT
* **Primary Weapons:** Twin Magnetic Chakrams (*Agni-Rings*) — razor-edged aerodynamic circular blades that spin at supersonic speeds and return to her magnetic wrist gauntlets.
* **Utility Tool:** Multi-frequency arc-welding torch and electromagnetic grappling line.

## 3. PERSONALITY & DEMEANOR
* Fierce, pragmatic, razor-witted, fiercely protective of her people, harboring deep hatred for Imperial dogma.

# CHARACTER DESIGN SHEET: ARYAN (AWAKENED ASCETIC)
**Role:** Protagonist / Wandering Warrior  
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W) — Crisp G-Pen, heavy black brushwork, kakeami crosshatching.

## 1. VISUAL ANATOMY & COSTUME
* **Physique:** Tall, sinewy, battle-hardened musculature covered in jagged war scars.
* **Head & Hair:** Long unkempt black hair bound in a warrior's topknot (*shikha*); stray bangs framing a severe, chiseled face with intense hooded eyes and permanent dark under-eye shadow (*kage*).
* **Mark of Awakening:** Fractal golden/cyan Astra-circuit veins glowing across his right shoulder, neck, and right arm.
* **Attire:** 
  * Tattered deep indigo/black ascetic dhoti wrapped around waist with hemp rope and brass prayer beads.
  * Ragged shoulder cowl draping across left shoulder, leaving right scarred combat arm exposed.
  * Heavy leather arm braces bound with inscribed protective mantras.
  * Straw-and-leather traveler sandals (*paduka*).

## 2. WEAPON & COMBAT ARSENAL
* **Weapon:** *Resonant Kanabo (Vajra-Staff)*
  * Description: A six-foot heavy octagonal iron monk staff studded with blunt conical rivets. The tip houses a glowing core socket that hums with kinetic lightning.
* **Combat Style:** Crushing kinetic strikes, 3D panel-breaking swings, heavy ground smashes that shatter stone, combined with agile monk evasion.

## 3. EXPRESSION MATRIX
1. **Wrathful Determination:** Gritted teeth, intense speed lines emanating from pupil, heavy brow shadow.
2. **Karmic Pain:** Straining jaw, beads of sweat and ink spray as the Astra-circuit burns into his skin.
3. **Calm Serenity (Zen Focus):** Narrowed composed eyes, single ink line contour, deep breath before striking.

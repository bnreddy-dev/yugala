# ENEMY DESIGN SHEET: KALA CHAKRA SKY LEGION
**Role:** Standard Imperial Combat Units  
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W)

## 1. VARIANTS
1. **Imperial Skiff Hunters:** Swift, light-armored scouts piloting jet-propelled sand skiffs, armed with repeating harpoon guns.
2. **Sky Legionnaires:** Heavy infantry with jump-jets and energy tower shields, executing disciplined phalanx formations.
3. **Astra-Snipers:** Positioned on high vantage points, wielding long-barreled plasma rifles with thermal scope lenses.

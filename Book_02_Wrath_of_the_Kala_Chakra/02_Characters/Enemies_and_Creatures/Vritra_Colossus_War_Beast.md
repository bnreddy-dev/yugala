# CREATURE / MECH SHEET: VRITRA (ASTRA-COLOSSUS)
**Role:** Act II Megastructure Boss  
**Visual Style:** Classic Manga Ink (B&W) — Monumental scale, intricate mechanical linework, heavy screentone texturing.

## 1. SPECIFICATIONS
* **Classification:** Class-IX Autonomous Siege Titan.
* **Height:** 120 meters.
* **Locomotion:** Six articulated armored leg-struts with continuous caterpillar treads for crushing terrain.
* **Armament:** Heavy dorsal Astra-Beam Cannon, rapid-fire flak turrets along its flanks, and massive seismic drill tusks.
* **Weak Point:** Central Exposed Reactor Core located within the thoracic vent behind triple-interlocking hydraulic blast doors.

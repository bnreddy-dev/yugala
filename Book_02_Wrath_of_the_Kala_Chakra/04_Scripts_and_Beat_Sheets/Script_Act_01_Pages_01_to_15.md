## PAGE 01
* **Layout:** 3 Panels.
* **Panel 1 (Wide High-Angle Splash):** The vast desolate rim of the Brahma Wound crater. Aryan stands on a jagged promontory, silhouetted against the dark apocalyptic horizon. In the far sky, dark shapes of imperial drop-ships descend through burning storm clouds.
  * *Caption:* "The wound of the gods never heals. It only festers."
* **Panel 2 (Close-up):** Aryan's face in deep profile. Hooded eyes, intense kage brow shadows. His right arm pulses with fractal luminous circuit-veins.
  * *SFX:* *THROB... THROB...*
* **Panel 3 (Medium Shot):** Aryan tightens his grip on the Resonant Kanabo. The studded iron staff sparks with kinetic arcs.

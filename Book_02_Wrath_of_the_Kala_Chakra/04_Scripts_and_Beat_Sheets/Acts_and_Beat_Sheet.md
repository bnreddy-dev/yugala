# ACTS & BEAT SHEET: BOOK 02

## ACT I: THE SCORCHED PURSUIT (PAGES 01–15)
* **Opening Image:** Aryan amidst the dust storm of the Brahma Wound crater.
* **Inciting Incident:** Imperial hunter skiffs deploy to eliminate any witnesses of the Astra awakening.
* **Rising Action:** Fierce wasteland chase and combat; introduction of Veda.
* **Plot Point 1:** Arrival at the subterranean Vedic Vaults; discovery of the Citadel's countdown to world reset.

## ACT II: THE TITAN & THE STRATOSPHERE (PAGES 16–35)
* **Midpoint Catalyst:** General Kripa attacks with the mountain-sized Vritra Colossus.
* **Action Climax:** Aryan and Veda climb and sabotage the Colossus from the inside.
* **Ticking Clock:** Hijacking an Imperial Sky-Barge to reach the sky citadel before the three golden rings align.

## ACT III: THE SIEGE OF ETERNITY (PAGES 36–50)
* **All Is Lost / Dark Night of Soul:** Aryan's Astra-veins reach critical burn; one more strike could crystallize him.
* **Climactic Battle:** Defeating General Kripa and confronting Emperor Vishwamitra.
* **Ending Cliffhanger:** The shattering of the Kala Chakra and the cosmic fracture across Aryavarta.
